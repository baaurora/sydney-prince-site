2. About page concepts

Each one keeps three photos around "Get to know Sydney!", breaks the long text up with photos on alternating sides, and adds illustrated objects (in the matchbook drawing style) that show creativity and logistics instead of telling.

Recommended

The Prompt Book

  • The page is Sydney's prompt book. Text sits on lined pages, and photos are taped in on alternating sides.
  • Red-pencil cue marks ("Q1 — GO") lead from one section to the next as you scroll, and each mark draws itself in.
  • "creativity" and "logistics" each get an illustrated vignette: a paintbrush and spotlight, then a clipboard checklist, headset and gaffer tape.
  • The organizations become small matchbooks from her collection, each labelled with the shows she did there (from her credits). That gives them the context they're missing now.
  • "Go Blue!" gets an illustrated Michigan pennant.

The Collection Drawer

  • A wooden type-drawer or shadow box. Photos and illustrated objects sit in compartments like a collector's display.
  • Each paragraph sits between two drawer rows, so the text is always broken up by something to look at.
  • Hovering a compartment lifts the item out, like taking a matchbook out of a collection.

The Cue Sheet

  • A red cue line runs down the page. Each paragraph is a numbered cue, with its photo on the opposite side.
  • Illustrated props are "preset" along the line: headset, clipboard, cue light, Michigan pennant.
  • A cue light at the top of the line turns green as you reach each cue.
