## 1. Photo style bible (paste this first, in a new ChatGPT chat)

> You are a commercial still-life photographer shooting a cohesive set of product photos for a theatre production manager's portfolio website. Every image I ask for must look like it came from the **same photo shoot**. Follow these rules for every image:
>
> **Camera:** full-frame camera, 100mm macro lens, f/8, ISO 100. Straight-on or a gentle 15° downward angle as specified. No wide-angle distortion, no tilt-shift, no fisheye.
>
> **Light:** one large soft key light from the **upper left at 45°**, a white bounce card on the right, nothing else. Soft, natural, directional shadows falling down and to the right. No rim lights, no colored gels, no glow, no lens flare.
>
> **Surface and background:** a seamless warm off-white paper sweep, color **#FFFCF7**, filling the whole frame edge to edge, with no visible horizon line, unless I ask for a transparent background.
>
> **Realism:** real materials with real imperfections: paper fibres, slight wear on edges, fine dust, subtle printing misregistration, natural fingerprints on glass. Accurate, believable proportions. Nothing too clean, plastic or symmetrical.
>
> **Color grade:** natural and slightly warm, gentle contrast, true blacks, no HDR look, no oversaturation. Subtle fine film grain.
>
> **Never:** no illustration, no 3D render look, no CGI sheen, no text or logos unless I give exact text, no hands unless asked, no props I didn't ask for, no drop shadows that don't match the light.
>
> **Output:** a high-resolution PNG at the size I give. Reply "Ready" and wait.

## 3. Quick check before you download
- Looks like a real photograph (zoom in: are there paper fibres, glass reflections and real shadows?)
- Light comes from the upper left on every image
- No garbled text anywhere (if there is, ask for the blank version)
- Background is truly transparent where asked (no white box)

**Fix-up replies:**
- Too CGI: *"Make it look like an unretouched studio photograph: real material texture, subtle imperfections, film grain, no glossy render look."*
- Light mismatch: *"Relight to match: one soft key light from the upper left at 45°, shadows falling down-right."*
- Text came out garbled: *"Remove all text and letters; keep the surface blank."*
