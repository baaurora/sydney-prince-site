## 1. Style bible (paste first)

> You are illustrating a set of small spot drawings for a theatre production manager's portfolio website. The site's theme is "a vintage venue matchbook from her collection": warm, collected, a little nostalgic, but polished and professional, never goofy. All images must look like they were drawn by the same illustrator with the same pen. Read these rules carefully and apply them to every image I request:
>
> **1. Line.** One consistent ink line, near-black `#1F1B18`, about 6–8 px thick on a 1024 px canvas. The line has a subtle hand-drawn waver, like a confident brush pen on paper, not a shaky or sketchy line. Lines are closed and clean, rounded ends, no hatching, no cross-hatching, no outlines doubled up.
>
> **2. Color.** Flat fills only, no gradients, no airbrush, no shading ramps, no glow. Use only this palette: cream `#F6EEDF`, paper white `#FBF6EC`, tomato red `#D9442B`, deep red `#B8381F`, striker brown `#8A6A4F`, dark brown `#6E533C`, ink `#1F1B18`. Mustard `#F2A33A` only as a tiny accent (a highlight, a lamp). At most 3 fill colors per object plus ink. To show form, use one flat darker shape of the same hue on the shadow side (e.g., deep red on tomato red), never a gradient.
>
> **3. Texture.** A very light, even paper/risograph grain over the colored areas only, as if printed on matchbook card stock. Fills may sit 1–2 px off the line (slight print misregistration). Keep it subtle.
>
> **4. Shapes.** Simplified, slightly chunky, friendly proportions, like 1950s matchbook and theatre-program illustration. Clear silhouettes that read at small sizes (the art will often display at 140–260 px wide). Minimal interior detail.
>
> **5. Lettering** (only when I ask for text). Hand-lettered bold, soft, slightly bouncy serif capitals in the style of Fraunces Soft or Recoleta. Even spacing, perfectly legible, spelled exactly as given.
>
> **6. Background and framing.** Transparent background (true alpha PNG, no white or checkerboard pixels). One object only, centered, with about 10% empty margin on every side. No ground shadow, no floor line, no scenery, no border, no frame, no signature, no watermark.
>
> **7. View.** A gentle three-quarter or straight-on view as specified per image. No dramatic perspective, no fisheye, no 3D render look.
>
> **8. Never.** No photorealism, no glossy highlights, no gradients, no drop shadows, no neon, no extra colors outside the palette, no cartoon faces on objects, no stickers or emoji look, no real brand logos.
>
> Reply "Ready" and wait for my first request.

## 2. Spot illustrations

Format for all six: **1024 × 1024 px PNG, transparent background**, unless noted.

### 2.1 `ill-headset.png`: top corner of the prompt book page
**On the page:** sits in the top-right corner of the paper "prompt book" page, about 140 px wide, slightly overlapping the page edge like it was set down on the book.
> Draw a professional stage manager's single-ear headset, three-quarter view from above. A padded ear cup in ink-black with a dark-brown cushion, a slim curved headband in ink, and a thin boom microphone arm curving forward with a small cream foam windscreen on the tip. A coiled cord in ink hangs down and loops once loosely. Keep it sleek and real-world accurate (like a ClearCom/Telex-style headset), not a gaming headset. Mostly ink with dark brown, one small tomato-red detail (a tiny red tally light on the ear cup).

### 2.2 `ill-creativity.png`: next to "creativity"
**On the page:** sits beside the paragraph that says her work lives at the intersection of creativity and logistics, paired side by side with the logistics drawing. Both display about 200 px wide, so they must feel like a matched pair (same scale, same weight, mirror-friendly composition).
> Draw a single composition that says "creativity" for theatre: a round wooden-handled paintbrush lying diagonally (bottom-left to top-right), its tip loaded with tomato-red paint, one fat red drip falling from the tip. Behind it, a small vintage theatre spotlight (a Fresnel-style lamp on a short yoke) pointing up-right, throwing a simple flat cream beam that widens out to the edge. Paintbrush handle in striker brown, ferrule in cream with ink lines, spotlight body in ink with a deep-red lens ring. Balanced in a square, objects overlapping slightly.

### 2.3 `ill-logistics.png`: the pair to creativity
**On the page:** right next to `ill-creativity.png`, same size. Compose it as the counterweight to the creativity drawing.
> Draw a single composition that says "logistics" for theatre: a clipboard with a striker-brown board and an ink-black metal clip, holding a paper-white sheet with a short checklist of 4 lines, each with a tomato-red checkmark (no readable words, just short ink lines). A yellow-mustard pencil tucked under the clip at an angle. A small round stopwatch in cream with an ink rim hangs from the clip's corner on a short loop. Straight-on view, the clipboard slightly tilted (about 6°) to the left.

### 2.4 `ill-pippin.png`: beside the family photo
**On the page:** sits at the bottom corner of the photo of Sydney, Delilah and Pippin, peeking over the frame edge, about 120 px wide. Pippin is their real puppy, so keep it sweet and specific, not generic.
> Draw a small, happy, scruffy puppy sitting and facing three-quarters toward the viewer, tongue out, ears soft and floppy. Curly, fluffy coat drawn as a few simple scalloped outline bumps (not hair strokes). Coat in cream with patches of light caramel (use striker brown at low intensity) and a dark-brown nose and eyes. Simple dot eyes with one tiny highlight, a little pink-red tongue in tomato red. Charming and minimal, like a mascot on a vintage matchbook. No collar text.

### 2.5 `ill-go-blue.png`: beside the University of Michigan line
**On the page:** floats beside the paragraph "I'm a proud University of Michigan alum (Go Blue!)", about 220 px wide, tilted slightly, as if pinned to the page.
**Palette exception:** this one uses navy `#1B2A4A` and maize `#F2C230` instead of red, plus ink and cream.
> Draw a vintage felt college pennant pointing to the right, in navy blue, with a maize-yellow stripe along the left edge where it would attach to a stick, and two small maize tassels/strings at the left. Across the pennant, hand-lettered in maize: "GO BLUE" in bold, soft, bouncy serif capitals with a thin ink outline. Do not include any university logo, block "M", seal or mascot. Add a subtle felt texture and one gentle wave in the fabric. A small brass-colored pushpin (use mustard and dark brown) in the top-left corner, as if pinned to a board.

### 2.6 `ill-spike-marks.png`: footer of the prompt book page
**On the page:** a wide, low decoration across the bottom of the page, like the stage floor under the text. **Format: 1600 × 400 px PNG, transparent.**
> Draw a row of five spike-tape marks as they appear on a theatre stage floor, laid out loosely left to right with generous space between them: a tomato-red "T", a cream "L" corner, a dark-brown "X", a small tomato-red "+", and a cream short dash. Each mark is a strip of tape with slightly torn, uneven ends and a thin ink outline, flat color, slightly rotated at different angles. No floor, no background, no shadows.

## 3b. The matchbook jar (entryway key jar)

**On the page:** Sydney's organizations are a pile of matchbooks inside a glass jar sitting on an entryway shelf, like the bowl by the front door where you toss your keys. Visitors click a matchbook to pull it out and read what she did there. The site already draws a simple jar. This illustration replaces that drawing; the matchbooks are layered on top by the site, so **the jar must be empty**.

**Filename:** `ill-jar.png` · **Format:** 1000 × 1100 px PNG, transparent background

> Draw an empty, clear glass fishbowl-style jar, straight-on front view, in the established style. Exact proportions matter because other images will be placed inside it:
> - The **opening (rim)** is an ellipse centered horizontally, its center at **17% from the top** of the canvas, **54% of the canvas width** wide, with a thin rolled glass lip.
> - The **body** swells out to **92% of the canvas width** at about 55% down, then curves in to a flat round base whose bottom sits at **92% from the top**.
> - Glass is shown with the ink outline plus two or three simple cream highlight strokes (a long curved one on the left side, a short one at upper right) and a very faint cool cream tint inside the body (about 15% opacity). The inside must be empty and mostly see-through.
> - No water, no fish, no keys, no table, no shadow under it.

**Optional, a cozy entryway behind the jar:** `ill-entryway.png`, 1600 × 900 px, transparent outside the drawing:
> A simple, flat entryway vignette in the established style, straight-on: a narrow wooden console shelf in striker brown across the bottom third, a row of three wall hooks above it holding a canvas tote, a ring of keys and a theatre lanyard with a blank badge, and a small framed show poster (blank) on the wall. Leave a clear empty space on the shelf, centered, 40% of the width, where a jar will be placed. Muted, uncluttered, no people.

## 4. QA checklist (check every image before you download it)

- [ ] Background is truly transparent (no white box when you place it on a dark slide)
- [ ] Only palette colors, no gradients, no glossy shine, no drop shadow
- [ ] Ink line weight matches the previous images in the chat
- [ ] Object is centered with breathing room; nothing touches the edge
- [ ] Any lettering is spelled exactly right
- [ ] It still reads clearly when you zoom out to thumbnail size
- [ ] No real logos

**Fix-up replies:**
- Too shiny or 3D: *"Flatter: flat fills only, no gradients or highlights, a single darker flat shape for shadow."*
- Line too thin or sketchy: *"Use one confident 7px ink line with a slight hand waver, no sketch lines, no hatching."*
- Style drifting from earlier images: *"Match the line weight, palette and grain of the headset image exactly."*
- White background: *"Re-export as a PNG with a fully transparent background."*
- Too detailed: *"Simplify to fewer shapes so it reads at 150 px wide."*
